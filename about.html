Our Mission

Why we built a digital sanctuary for our peers.

Dismantling Stigma

Mental health challenges are heavily stigmatised in many Zimbabwean communities, forcing too many young people to carry anxiety, depression, and stress in absolute silence.

The Safehouse Zim was engineered to break this barrier. While we do not employ our own medical practitioners or licensed professionals yet, this hub functions as a clean, highly accessible, zero-friction path directly to verified, professional national helplines completely for free.
