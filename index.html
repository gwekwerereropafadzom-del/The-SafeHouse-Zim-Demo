Advocacy & Resources

Breaking the stigma around youth mental health in Zimbabwe. We aren't doctors, but we believe no one should struggle in silence. Here are free, confidential local spaces built for us.

Youth Advocates Helpline 📞 Dial 393 or WhatsApp +263 777 469 107. Free, 24/7 support for mental health, stress, and youth issues.
→
Childline Zimbabwe 💜 Dial 116 or WhatsApp +263 719 116 116. Free, multilingual crisis support and child safety resources.
→
The Friendship Bench 🌿 World-renowned community-led talk therapy. Connect safely online or via WhatsApp.
→

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service completely free?

Yes. All the helpline resources, toll-free shortcodes, and WhatsApp numbers listed on this hub are 100% free of charge to access from within Zimbabwe.

Are these chats confidential?

Absolutely. Organizations like Childline and Youth Advocates operate under strict privacy protocols. Your conversations are anonymous and safe.

Who runs The Safehouse Zim?

This platform was built independently by a Zimbabwean AS-Level Computer Science student as a community resource and advocacy framework to dismantle mental wellness stigma.
